Four ways to move forward

WT

Workforce Training

Learn a trade or earn a certification that leads directly to a job. Examples: Welding, HVAC, CNA, CDL.

3–9 months
CC

Community College

Earn an associate degree or transfer to a university. Affordable and local.

2 years
UN

University

Earn a bachelor's degree or higher. Offers broad career options and higher earning potential.

4 years, or 2 after transfer
AE

Adult Education

Improve English, complete your GED, or build foundational skills to enter college or job training.

Ongoing, flexible

⚠️ IMPORTANT / IMPORTANTE

Texas Licensing Alert: Some workforce pathways lead to careers that require a state license. Texas now requires documentation establishing lawful presence for certain professional licenses regulated by TDLR. Before enrolling in a training program, check whether your chosen pathway requires licensure and whether additional eligibility rules apply.

Alerta de Licencias en Texas: Algunos caminos de capacitación conducen a carreras que requieren una licencia estatal. Texas ahora exige documentación que establezca presencia legal para ciertas licencias profesionales reguladas por TDLR. Antes de inscribirse en un programa, verifique si la carrera que le interesa requiere licencia y si aplican requisitos adicionales.
