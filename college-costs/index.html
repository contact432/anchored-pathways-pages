A fairer comparison

The small details
make a difference.

Read these notes before deciding one school is cheaper than another.

Texas resident is not the same as in district.

A Harris County address does not automatically qualify you for a college’s lowest tuition rate. Ask the college to confirm your taxing-district status, tuition classification and any approved waiver. FAFSA/TASFA and tuition residency are separate checks.

Not every school uses the same credit load.

Several local estimates use 30 credits annually. UT Austin and SFA use 12 credits per term; UST publishes a full-time range. A&M’s cited general estimate does not specify a credit load. Ask how your planned courses affect annual cost and time to completion.

A housing budget is not necessarily your savings.

Living at home still has costs, and changing your housing plan can change aid. If your family remains in Harris County, a “with parents” budget at Austin or Nacogdoches only applies if you actually have family housing near that campus.

Why we do not rank all schools by sticker price.

The figures differ in course load, housing assumptions and completeness. UH’s admissions flyer is a partial subtotal; A&M and San Jac publish general estimates without the housing split used here. Compare actual offers after confirmed grants and scholarships, not loans.
