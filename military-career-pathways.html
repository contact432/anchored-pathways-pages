Ways In

Four routes into service

Cuatro rutas para ingresar al servicio

Every path below leads to the same place — education benefits after service. The difference is when college happens, and whether a student enters as an officer or enlists first.

Cada camino lleva al mismo lugar: beneficios educativos después del servicio. La diferencia está en cuándo ocurre la universidad y si el estudiante ingresa como oficial o se alista primero.

Officer · College First

ROTC — Reserve Officers' Training Corps

ROTC — Cuerpo de Entrenamiento de Oficiales de Reserva

Attend college full-time while training to become a military officer. Graduate with a degree and a commission.

Asista a la universidad a tiempo completo mientras se entrena para ser oficial militar. Se gradúa con un título y un nombramiento como oficial.

  • Enroll at a college with an Army, Navy/Marine Corps, Air Force, or Space Force ROTC program
  • Complete coursework and physical training alongside regular classes
  • Scholarships can cover up to 100% of tuition and fees, plus a monthly stipend
  • Apply junior or senior year of high school — deadlines vary by branch

Best fit for students who

  • Want a degree AND military service
  • Are drawn to leadership and management roles
  • Need financial help paying for college
Enlisted · Full-Time

Enlisted Service

Servicio Alistado

Join directly after high school. Pay, housing, healthcare, and job training start day one — plus education benefits to use later.

Únase directamente después de la escuela secundaria. El salario, la vivienda, la atención médica y la capacitación laboral comienzan desde el primer día, además de beneficios educativos para usar más adelante.

  • 8–12 weeks of basic training (varies by branch)
  • Assigned a specific job or skill area (MOS)
  • Typical commitment: 4 years active duty
  • Tuition Assistance: up to $4,500/year for college courses while serving

Six branches to research

  • Army, Navy, Marine Corps, Air Force, Space Force, Coast Guard — each has different jobs, culture, and deployment patterns
Part-Time · Close to Home

National Guard & Reserves

Guardia Nacional y Reservas

Serve part-time — about one weekend a month and two weeks a year — while living and working in your community.

Sirva a tiempo parcial, aproximadamente un fin de semana al mes y dos semanas al año, mientras vive y trabaja en su comunidad.

  • Texas Hazlewood Act: Texas National Guard members may qualify for up to 150 credit hours of tuition exemption at Texas public colleges
  • One of the most valuable state education benefits in the country
  • Available to eligible veterans, active duty, and Guard/Reserve members who are Texas residents

Best fit for students who

  • Want to stay close to home and community
  • Want education benefits without a full-time commitment
  • Are drawn to emergency response or state-level roles
Officer · After a Degree

OCS — Officer Candidate School

OCS — Escuela de Candidatos a Oficial

The path to becoming an officer for college graduates who didn't do ROTC. Enlist, complete OCS, receive a commission.

El camino para convertirse en oficial para quienes ya tienen un título universitario y no hicieron ROTC. Se alista, completa la OCS y recibe un nombramiento como oficial.

  • Must have a bachelor's degree (any major)
  • Meet age, fitness, and background requirements per branch
  • Program length typically 10–17 weeks

Best fit for students who

  • Already have or are finishing a degree
  • Want officer status and leadership responsibility
  • Didn't do ROTC but want to serve

After Service

Military jobs that transition best to civilian careers

Empleos militares que mejor se transforman en carreras civiles

Every military job builds transferable skills, but some fields map onto civilian careers more directly — based on U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics data comparing military and civilian occupation codes.

Todo empleo militar desarrolla habilidades transferibles, pero algunos campos se relacionan más directamente con carreras civiles, según datos de la Oficina de Estadísticas Laborales de EE. UU.

Cybersecurity & IT

Ciberseguridad y TI

Cyber and IT roles (Army 17-series, Navy IT, Air Force cyber defense) lead to information security analyst and network administrator jobs.

Estos puestos conducen a empleos como analista de seguridad de la información y administrador de redes.

Projected 33% growth

Healthcare

Atención Médica

Combat medics and corpsmen translate to EMT or paramedic — and with more schooling, registered nurse or physician assistant.

Los médicos de combate se convierten en técnicos de emergencias médicas o, con más estudios, en enfermeros registrados.

Nurse practitioner: 46% growth

Logistics & Supply Chain

Logística y Cadena de Suministro

Supply and logistics roles across every branch map almost one-to-one to supply chain coordinator, logistician, and operations roles.

Estos puestos se relacionan casi directamente con roles de coordinador de cadena de suministro y logística.

Skilled Trades

Oficios Especializados

Construction, electrical, and mechanical training leads to licensed trade work — often with credit toward apprenticeship hours. Strong pay, no student debt required.

La capacitación en construcción, electricidad y mecánica conduce a trabajos de oficio con licencia, con buena paga y sin deuda estudiantil.

Law Enforcement & Security

Aplicación de la Ley y Seguridad

Military Police and Security Forces roles translate directly to civilian police work, corporate security, and federal protective services.

Se traducen directamente en trabajo policial civil, seguridad corporativa y servicios de protección federal.

Leadership & Operations

Liderazgo y Operaciones

Even roles without a direct civilian match — like infantry — build leadership, planning, and team-management experience valued across every industry.

Desarrollan experiencia en liderazgo y gestión de equipos, valorada en toda industria.

Free tool: the O*NET Military Crosswalk lets a student search their exact military job code (MOS, AFSC, or rating) for a personalized list of matching civilian careers, required education, and typical pay. onetonline.org/crosswalk/MOC

Herramienta gratuita: busque el código de empleo militar exacto para obtener una lista personalizada de carreras civiles equivalentes.

The Payoff

GI Bill & education benefits after service

GI Bill y beneficios educativos después del servicio

Veterans who complete their service obligation qualify for benefits that can cover college, trade school, certificate programs, and more.

Los veteranos que completan su obligación de servicio califican para beneficios que pueden cubrir la universidad, la escuela vocacional y más.

Post-9/11 GI Bill (Ch. 33)

  • 100% of tuition and fees at public in-state schools
  • Monthly housing allowance + book/supply stipend
  • Up to 36 months of benefits (~4 academic years)
  • Can transfer to a spouse or dependent child in some cases

Cubre el 100% de la matrícula y cuotas, incluye una asignación mensual de vivienda y hasta 36 meses de beneficios.

Montgomery GI Bill (Ch. 30)

  • Monthly stipend depending on enrollment and training type
  • Requires a $1,200 contribution during service to activate

Estipendio mensual que depende del tipo de inscripción; requiere una contribución de $1,200 durante el servicio.

Vocational Rehabilitation (Ch. 31)

  • For veterans with a service-connected disability
  • Covers education, training, and employment services

Para veteranos con una discapacidad relacionada con el servicio; cubre educación, capacitación y servicios de empleo.

Before You Decide

Questions worth asking

Preguntas que vale la pena hacer

  • ?What job or career am I interested in? Does the military offer training in that area?

    ¿Qué trabajo o carrera me interesa? ¿Ofrece el ejército capacitación en esa área?

  • ?Am I ready for a multi-year commitment right after high school, or do I want college first?

    ¿Estoy listo para un compromiso de varios años justo después de la secundaria, o prefiero la universidad primero?

  • ?What branch fits my personality and goals?

    ¿Qué rama se ajusta a mi personalidad y metas?

  • ?What happens to my education if I enlist? What benefits will I have?

    ¿Qué pasa con mi educación si me alisto? ¿Qué beneficios tendré?

  • ?How does deployment affect my family and plans?

    ¿Cómo afecta el despliegue a mi familia y mis planes?

  • ?What do veterans in my community say about their experience?

    ¿Qué dicen los veteranos de mi comunidad sobre su experiencia?
